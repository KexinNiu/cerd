iGEM · Comparative Environmental Response Database

What the first extraction pass actually found

175 stress-response measurements pulled from 24 open-access papers by an LLM extractor, then re-checked against the source text. This page exists to show the team where the data supports a dose–response curve and where it does not.

Unverified draft data. Every record here was extracted by a language model and has verified_by: null. No organism curator has signed off yet, so nothing on this page is releasable — it is a look at the pipeline's output, not a result.

UV dose–response

measured author-derived

Dose converted to J/m² and survival to percent of the untreated control by pipeline/normalize.py. Both axes are logarithmic — UV inactivation spans orders of magnitude. Each point is one measurement at one dose; hover for the strain, assay and source.

Temperature response

measured author-derived

Survival against exposure temperature in °C. The spread is far thinner than the UV side: most temperature papers report lifespan in days or growth rate per hour, neither of which is a survival percentage.

Coverage by cell

All 175 records, including the ones that cannot go on the axes above. The levels column counts distinct doses or temperatures — a cell with one level cannot produce a curve no matter how many records it holds.
